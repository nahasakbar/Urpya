Every kind of debt

How it works

Step 1

Add your debts

Enter what you borrowed, what you pay each month and any interest. Kaayi works out what’s left.

Step 2

Record what you pay

Each month, note your payments. Kaayi shows what’s due, what’s overdue and how far you’ve come.

Step 3

Follow your plan

Set a monthly budget, and Kaayi shows which debt to pay first and when you’ll be debt-free.

Kaayi helps you think things through. It isn’t financial advice, so check anything important with your lender before acting on it.
