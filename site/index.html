Kaayi

A personal tracker for debts and income, with a plan to be debt-free. For bank loans, credit cards and the money you owe to family alike.

  • A month-by-month plan to be debt-free, and when you’ll get there
  • Income from work, a business or rent, kept alongside
  • Share a single debt with the people you manage it with
